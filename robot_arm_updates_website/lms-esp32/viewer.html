Rows count from the USB-C end. GND and 3V3 are the first pair of pins; IO32 is four rows further down the inner column, straight across from IO33. IO32 is on ADC1, which still reads while Wi-Fi is on.

The MyoWare runs on 2.9–5.7 V and its SIG output swings 0 V to its supply, so on 3V3 it stays inside the ESP32's ADC range. Its electrode pads snap straight onto the two studs underneath, and the third goes on the reference lead.

The sensor's output centres on 1.5 V and swings 0–3.0 V, inside the ESP32's 3.3 V ADC range. DFRobot rates the board for 3.3–5.5 V at 20 mA or more.

SPIKE hub

Anton's SPIKE cable runs from the LMS-ESP32's 2 × 3 HUB header to hub port A. Through it the hub powers the board and the two talk over serial: IO8 and IO7 on the board to the port's ID1 and ID2 lines. M+ and M− carry the port's motor lines; 3V3 and GND are shared. The lid's cutout over the HUB header lets the plug through with the lid on.

Electrodes

Three snap leads, red, blue and black, on one 3.5 mm TRS plug. They're shown short and laid straight here; the real leads are about a metre long.

LMS-ESP32 v2.0

39.8 × 55.8 mm, the size of a LEGO Technic 5 × 7 frame. ESP32-PICO-MINI-02, CH340C USB-serial, USB-C, the 14 × 2 header with both rows labelled, the HUB box header for SPIKE, Grove, Qwiic and the 10-pin display socket. Parts placed from Anton's top-view photo.

Printed case

GTAC-Science's case (Thingiverse 7032044, CC BY-SA): base, lid and RST button cap. Its floor holes take LEGO pins through the board's own holes, and the lid leaves the header open for the Dupont plugs.

MyoWare muscle sensor

Advancer Technologies AT-04-001 (Pololu 2732), 53.3 × 20.8 mm, red: electrode snaps underneath, a 3 in reference lead, and a 1 × 3 male header in its + − SIG holes for the Dupont leads. Labelled as printed, upside down as on the real board.

Gravity EMG sensor

DFRobot SEN0240 signal board, 35 × 22 mm: 3.5 mm electrode jack on one end, the Gravity PH2.0 socket on the other, labelled − + A.

SPIKE Prime hub

LEGO 45601, 7 × 11 × 4 studs (56 × 88 × 32 mm): white top, yellow base, six LPF2 ports, A/C/E down one side and B/D/F down the other. Geometry from Philippe Hurbain's LDraw part (CC BY 4.0); the port letters and the 5 × 5 light matrix are drawn on.

SPIKE Prime box

The storage box from set 45678, 41.3 × 30.3 × 15.5 cm, without the sorting trays or stickers: a yellow tapered tub with a rolled rim and grab handles on the short ends, and a white lid that drops inside the rim, with a raised panel on top. Wall thickness and taper are estimated from photos. Three 2 × 2 inch stickers sit side by side on the front: QR codes that join Wi-Fi networks GTAC 1, GTAC 2 and GTAC 3 (password robotarm), each with its name under it.

Cables

The SPIKE cable: 2 × 3 Dupont housing to an LPF2 plug (13.9 × 7.5 × 13.7 mm, 5.85 mm of it inside the port). The Gravity cable: white PH2.0 plug to three single Dupont plugs. The snap electrodes follow the Plastics One 222 drawing: 14.1 mm head, 5.1 mm thick, 4 mm stud socket.
