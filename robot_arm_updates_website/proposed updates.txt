**Robotic arm instructions**
_____________________________

We will be connecting to WiFi unique to your LEGO hub to control your robotic arm. Look for the number on your hub and scan its QR code on your yellow tub using the camera app. 

When you connect to the wifi, an app will automatically open with controls that you can use to test your build. 

***You will get disconnected from the internet when you are connected to your robotic arm.
****Include this detail on the page displayed from the LMS ESP32

To reconnect, open the wifi settings on your iPad and reconnect to the "Discovery" network.
****Include this detail on the page displayed from the LMS ESP32 - toast notification when you click back to HOME that tells you to change network back to discovery with an appropriate screenshot.

Watch this video to learn how to open the app, and navigate to the remote controls.

------------------

Use the controller on your iPad to test it. Click on the joystick icon on the Mindstorms app to open the controls.

_______________________________
