It's not moving?

  1. Press the centre button to wake the hub.
  2. Press left or right until the hub shows program 0.
  3. Press the centre button again to start it. The hub shows a motor letter or R.
  4. Wi-Fi off? Hold left and right together for one second.
  5. Still stuck? Check the board's cable is in port A and the motors are in ports B to F.
