04/05

Connect your iPad to the arm

  1. On the iPad, open Settings → Wi-Fi.
  2. Join the network with your board's name, for example GTAC 12. The password is robotarm.
  3. A page pops up: tap Open the remote. Then tap Done at the top (not Cancel).
  4. No pop-up? Open the Robot arm app on your iPad's home screen.

The iPad may say there is no internet on this Wi-Fi. That is fine: stay connected. Switch back to the Discovery Wi-Fi when you finish.

05/05

Drive your arm with the remote

  1. Make sure the Remote tab and Basic are selected.
  2. Hold the D-pad (1) or push the slider (2) to move the motors.
  3. Tap ✎ Change remote to add, move or set up controls and choose which motor each one moves.

Want to code your arm instead? See Change how your arm moves.
